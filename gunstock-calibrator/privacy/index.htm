It measures position and rotation offsets of a controller mounted on a gunstock, while you see the real world through passthrough. Information we collect The app does not collect any personally identifiable information and does not send any data off the device. The app does not connect to the internet. There is no account or sign-in. The app uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Passthrough camera images are displayed by the Quest operating system. The app does not receive or store camera images. Information used only on the device While the app is running, it uses the position and rotation of the headset and controllers (tracking data) to calculate the offsets. Measurement results, and the tracking data used to calculate them, are saved as a CSV file in the app's own folder on the headset. This file never leaves the device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Each time you measure, the app copies the six offset values (numbers only) to the device clipboard so you can paste them into other apps. The app never sends clipboard contents anywhere. Sharing with third parties Because the app collects no information, it shares none with third parties. The Meta Horizon Store and the Meta Quest operating system handle information under Meta's own privacy policy. Children The app is not directed to children. Please use it only if you meet the minimum age in the Meta Quest terms of service. Changes If this policy changes, this page will be updated together with the "Last updated" date.
